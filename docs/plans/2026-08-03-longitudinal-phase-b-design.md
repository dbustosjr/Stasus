# Longitudinal Phase B Design

Approved direction from prior session. Builds on Phase A.

## Scope

1. **Camera dual-mode** — Opt-in form/stability feedback only for modes with strong signals (`face_gaze_hold`, `pose_habituation`). All camera exercises also offer preview + timer (+ manual count) without form scoring. Honest copy: not a form grade, not medical clearance.
2. **Calm tools** — Guided steppers for fear-avoidance, checking reframes, and grounding (breathing already interactive). Completing a guide stamps calm activity.
3. **Landing trust copy** — Accurate, careful language; no overclaim on camera correctness/safety or clinical outcomes.

## Out of scope

Care-team portal, aggregate ETL, partner row exports, EEG/BCI.
